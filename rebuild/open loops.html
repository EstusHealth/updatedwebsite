Open Loops

Your brain is holding too many windows open. Let's get them onto one screen.

An open loop is anything unfinished your brain keeps quietly running in the background. A text you have not sent. A form you have not filled. A feeling you have not named. Hold enough of them and everything feels loud.

How this works:

  1. Tick the loops below that feel true today. You do not have to type a thing. Recognising is easier than remembering.
  2. Add your own at the bottom of the list if something is missing.
  3. For each one in your entry, pick a tiny next step, park it for later, or close it. All three count.
  4. Copy or save your entry, or just screenshot it. Then let your brain put it down.
Nothing here is saved or sent anywhere. It lives on your screen only.
STEP 1 Tick what feels true today

Click any line to add it to your entry. Click again to remove it.

STEP 2 Your journal entry

This is your one page for today. Decide one small thing for each loop.

STEP 3 Put it down

The Markdown template drops into Obsidian, Notion, or any notes app with blank fields to fill in, so your loops become a real journal entry you can keep working on.

No streaks here. No guilt. Do this once a month or once a year and it still works. The point is the unload, not the habit.
