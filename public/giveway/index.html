👁 STEP 1 — SPOT THE HAZARD
Look at the driver's view and click the thing that could hurt you.

The cyan car with the white outline is YOU. Lettered cars are everyone else. Dashed arrows show where each car wants to go. On 👁 hazard scenarios, click the danger in the driver's view first. Keys: A B C D to answer, SPACE for the next scenario.

Why an OT built a road rules game

Driving is one of the most complex everyday occupations there is. It asks you to scan, predict, decide, and act, all at once, while the scene keeps moving. Plenty of learners know the rules on paper and still freeze at a live intersection, because reading a page is nothing like reading a road.

So this is deliberately not a written test. Every scenario gives you the picture first, from above and from the driver's seat, then asks what you would do. Hazard rounds ask you to find the danger before you choose. That is the skill that transfers to the car.

Play it in short bursts. Getting one wrong here is free, and the explanation tells you the rule and where it comes from.
